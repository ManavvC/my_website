Overview

Undergraduate Research Support Association

URSA (Undergraduate Research Support Association) is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting research opportunities and academic excellence among undergraduate students. Our mission is to connect students with faculty mentors, provide resources for research development, and create a community of young scholars dedicated to advancing knowledge in their fields.

The document below contains comprehensive information about our organization, including research opportunities, funding resources, and guidelines for getting involved in undergraduate research projects.
