Hi, I'm Manav! I'm a UIUC engineering student who is passionate about the future of robotics and manufacturing. I've had the chance to direct the development of "NovoPrint," an award-winning 6-DOF robotic arm capable of 3D printing. I explored this interest further during my internship at Formlabs, where I designed a 3-axis gantry system for advanced SLA printer testing. When I'm not bringing complex machines to life, I'm usually strategizing over a chessboard as a FIDE-rated player. If you're into robotics and making things, I'd love to chat!
